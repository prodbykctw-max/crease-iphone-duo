About CREASE in VR

CREASE VR is neon air hockey against the CPU, first to 7, in the Meta Quest browser. Pick your paddle hand and enter VR. Play with a controller (you feel the hits through haptics), with hand tracking (the paddle follows your index finger; pinch to start or rematch), or just by looking where you want the paddle to go.

It is free and opens straight from this page: no app store, no download, no account. On a computer, Desktop practice lets you play with the mouse.

The same game runs on phones, made for the iPhone Duo: open it for two players, one on each side; close it for you vs the CPU. Two players in different places can also play online.
