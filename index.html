    HOW TO PLAY

    • Drag your paddle. Stay on your side of the crease (the glowing center line). First to 7 wins.
    • Portrait: pocket table, ideal for solo.
    • Landscape: wide table, with one player on each side.
    • Rotate or fold mid-match — the table adapts and your score carries over.
    • SMASH: flick your paddle hard into the puck. It goes white-hot and faster.
    • BUMP: the gold bumpers on the crease kick the puck. Score off one for a BANK SHOT.
    • Power-ups spawn on the crease. Knock the puck through one to take it:
      BIG paddle · MULTI puck · WALL over your goal
    • FIRE MODE: score 3 goals in a row and your paddle, the puck and your side of the rails catch fire for 7 seconds.
    • ARENA POWERS: every world drops its own power orb on the centre line. Hit it to claim it: Midnight shooting star (every hit smashes), Toxic acid rain (their paddle shrinks), Ice deep freeze, Inferno lava ball, Ultraviolet blacklight, Miami riptide, Gold Rush golden goal (counts double), Void black hole, Sakura blossom wall, KCTW bass drop.
    • CHALLENGE A FRIEND: tap it on the menu, or after a match to send your final score, and share the link.
    • Tip: Share → Add to Home Screen for true full-screen.
    • Your settings and stats stay on this device. Anonymous play data may be used to improve CREASE. No names or contact details are collected.
