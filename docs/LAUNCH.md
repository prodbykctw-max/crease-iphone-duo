# CREASE launch plan

## First milestone
Reach 100 testers; measure completed matches and rematches before spending on ads.
The first release keeps solo, two-player, and all existing power-ups free.

## Hardware visit (call the store first)
Ask whether the specific iPhone Duo is on display and whether staff permit opening a browser game and filming your test. Availability and demo-device restrictions are unverified.
1. Open the game in Safari; start solo and confirm sound starts after the tap.
2. Test Easy, Normal and Hard. Check paddle tracking near screen edges.
3. Play two-player with two fingers moving simultaneously on opposite halves.
4. Record the score, fold/unfold during a rally, and check score and controls survive. Repeat during countdown, goal animation, pause, and results.
5. Rotate both ways; inspect buttons, safe areas, and accidental browser gestures.
6. Try rematch, challenge sharing, and returning to the page.
7. Record device model, OS/browser version, display mode, and each failure. Film only with permission, avoiding bystanders.
Do not describe the game as physically tested until these checks pass. A browser viewport simulation is not hardware verification.

## Seven-day promotion experiment
- Day 1: record a real head-to-head match; lead with action in the first two seconds.
- Day 2: publish a close-match clip with the playable link.
- Day 3: show the multi-puck power-up and player reactions.
- Day 4: post a short creator story: why the shared-screen experience matters.
- Day 5: invite a small set of relevant mobile gaming/foldable creators to test it personally.
- Day 6: ask testers about control problems and whether they played again.
- Day 7: compare visits, match starts, completions, rematches, and inquiry clicks.
Use ?src=instagram, ?src=tiktok, ?src=youtube, ?src=creator or ?src=store on the play URL. These measure arrival events, not unique people or cross-session attribution.

## Monetization experiments
1. Custom game commissions: existing Instagram inquiry link is the initial lead channel.
2. Sponsored arena: scope artwork, usage term, approval rounds and price for each client.
3. Optional premium arena/effects bundle: test willingness to pay $2.99–$4.99 once. These are proposed experiments, not validated prices.
Payments and paid content are not implemented in this change. Choose the content and payment provider before building checkout. Keep entitlements server-side once paid content exists.
