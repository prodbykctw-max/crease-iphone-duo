# CREASE — Launch Campaign

**Brand:** CREASE by KCTW  
**Handle:** @playcrease  
**Core line:** One screen. Two players. First to 7.  
**Campaign goal:** Make CREASE the game people send to the person sitting beside them.

## The promise

CREASE is a fast, free, one-screen competitive game. A match takes minutes,
the controls are obvious, and three straight goals trigger Fire Mode. The
visual signature is the single animated centerpiece on the crease: a living sun,
planet, crystal, relic, moon, or KCTW Sanctuary core shared by both players.

Do not market CREASE as “another mobile game.” Market the moment: **hand your
phone to a friend, turn it sideways, and settle it.**

## Two launch tracks

### Track A — Start now: Season Zero

Publicly launch the browser game now. The goal is proof: plays, rematches,
shares, screenshots, comments, and a small group of players who return.

- Landing link: `https://prodbykctw-max.github.io/crease-iphone-duo/`
- Primary CTA: **Play free. Challenge somebody beside you.**
- Content source: real game captures, not stock gameplay.
- Budget: $0 organic campaign.

### Track B — Foldable-device moment

When Apple or another phone maker publicly announces compatible hardware,
release a new “built for two” clip within 24 hours. Do not promise an official
partnership or use device branding in a way that suggests one. The existing game
already works on normal phones; the foldable conversation becomes an extra wave,
not the launch dependency.

## Video rules

- Format: 1080 × 1920, 24–35 seconds maximum; 6–10 seconds is better for a
  first hook.
- First frame must show a puck, a moving centerpiece, a goal, or Fire Mode.
- Use large subtitles because many viewers watch muted.
- Never open a clip with a menu or loading screen.
- End with: **PLAY FREE · LINK IN BIO** or **SEND THIS TO YOUR RIVAL**.
- Capture real gameplay. The visual proof matters more than an explanation.

## Creator outreach

Build a list of 50 small creators: mobile gaming, retro gaming, synthwave,
phone-tech, Atlanta creators, and friends who post competitive game clips.
Start with people whose posts regularly get comments, not only people with large
follower counts.

**DM template:**

> Yo — I built a free one-screen two-player game called CREASE. You and a friend
> play on opposite halves of one phone; first to 7, and three straight goals
> triggers Fire Mode. It’s browser-playable, no download. Want an early link to
> run a match on camera?

Do not mass-send all 50 at once. Send 10 personal messages, learn which clip
gets replies, then improve the next 10.

## Launch-day sequence

1. Publish the intro Reel and TikTok at 12 PM local time.
2. Put the game link in Stories with `PLAY FREE`.
3. Send the clip directly to 20 real people who may actually play or repost.
4. Post the same clip to personal `@prodbykctw` Stories, directing people to
   `@playcrease`.
5. Reply to every early comment quickly and ask a simple question: `Who are you
   challenging?`
6. Publish the Fire Mode clip 6–8 hours later.
7. Record the numbers, not just views.

## Scoreboard

Track these weekly in one note or spreadsheet:

| Metric | Healthy early signal |
|---|---|
| Link clicks | Rising after each clip |
| First match complete | Players are not bouncing immediately |
| Rematches | The core loop is working |
| Challenge shares | The growth loop is working |
| Fire Mode activations | Players understand the reward | 
| Follows per 1,000 views | The brand is memorable |
| Comments that tag a friend | The concept is social |

Do not spend money on ads until a post gets organic comments, shares, and real
challenge behavior. Use the winning organic clip as the future ad creative if
you choose to spend later.
